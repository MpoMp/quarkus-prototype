
# ADR-6: Model class mapping

## Status

**Status:** Active

**Proposed by:** Michalis Bogdanos

**Authored by:** Michalis Bogdanos

## Context

_What is the issue that we're seeing that is motivating this decision or change?_ 

A standard way of mapping classes to one another.

## Decision

_What is the change that we're proposing and/or doing?_ 

We will use https://mapstruct.org/. 

## Consequences

### Pros 

_List the advantages of the decision here._

- Using a library that is well-adopted.

### Cons

_List the disadvantages of the decision here._

- Somewhat locked to a specific mapper library.
